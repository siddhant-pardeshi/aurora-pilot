# How to play Aurora Pilot

## Objective

Fly the mail run as far as the light takes you. Rings, streaks, distance and briefings all feed the score; your best score is saved on this device. Three rock strikes — or an empty tank — end the flight.

## The HUD

- **Speed** — your airspeed. Climbing costs speed; diving earns it back.
- **Altitude** — your height in meters above the sea.
- **Ring streak** — consecutive rings without a miss; each is worth more.
- **Fuel** — the tank. It drains constantly, faster while boosting.

## The four skies

Every sixty seconds the palette crossfades: **Dawn → Bright day → Dusk → Starry night**, then the loop restarts. Each shift triggers a **flight briefing** — one concept card about the network layer your route just crossed. Briefings pause the flight for a breath; correct answers pay +250.

## Reading the canyon

- **Gold rings** are the economy: +10 each, multiplied by your streak. They spawn in gentle arcs — set your altitude early and fly the line, don't chase.
- **Stone spires and arches** are the tax: a hit costs speed, shakes the hull, and Skye winces. Three hits ground you.
- **Parachute canisters** drift down slowly. Fly through the canopy for +25% fuel. When the tank passes 25%, hunt them deliberately.
- **Bird flocks** are cosmetic — but skimming one pays a close-call bonus. So does threading a spire gap closely. Risk pays, misjudging doesn't.

## Energy management (the actual skill)

Climbing steeply bleeds airspeed; below stall speed the nose gets heavy. The smooth line is a sawtooth: climb on the ring arcs, dive on the gaps, and never fight the plane. Boosting is a burst — two seconds, fuel-hungry, perfect for reaching a high ring chain, wasteful anywhere else.

## Failure and recovery

- **Rock strike:** heavy shake, speed loss, hull −1. Straighten and breathe; the canyon is long.
- **Fuel out:** the engine goes quiet and the plane glides down. The run ends softly at the water — no crash, no shame.
- **Third hull hit:** results board. Your rings, streak and distance are tallied.

## Strategy quick-reference

1. Rings spawn where the eye wants to go — trust the arc, make tiny corrections.
2. After every briefing, re-check the fuel gauge. It keeps draining while you read.
3. Streaks are worth more than rings: a x4 streak turns +10 into +40. Protect it over greedy shortcuts.
4. At night, rings glow hardest against the ridgeline — fly the ridge.
